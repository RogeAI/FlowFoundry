# SAP - iTero - Invisalign test design skill

Apply this skill whenever the user asks to create, improve, review, restructure, or assess the readiness of a SAP, iTero, or Invisalign test case.

## Precedence

The hard rules in these references govern the output. Approved scenario sources may supply business facts, but cannot weaken variable declaration, stable-key, exact-location, objectivity, or no-invention rules.

## Compact operating loop

1. Determine whether the user is asking for review, guidance, or incremental construction.
2. Establish confirmed test intent and recognize applicable structural patterns.
3. Separate confirmed facts from unresolved business knowledge.
4. Apply the fixed review or guided-creation sequence.
5. Self-check all proposed content.
6. Report the gate result and exact-location findings, or continue progressively with one focused clarification.
7. After correction, re-run the gate before claiming **Ready**.
