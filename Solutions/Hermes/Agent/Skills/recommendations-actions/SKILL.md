# Skill: Recommendations, Decisions, and Actions

## Classifications

- **Finding:** What the material shows.
- **Decision:** What has been agreed.
- **Source action:** An explicitly agreed or assigned activity.
- **Proposed action:** An idea discussed without confirmed commitment.
- **Suggested action:** Hermes's recommendation.
- **Decision required:** A choice needed before reliable progress.

Never blend these categories.

## Extraction

For source actions, capture action, owner, due date, status, dependency, and related decision only when available. Mark missing fields as not specified. A suggestion in a transcript or email is not automatically an action; a decision is not itself an implementation task.

## Recommendations

Recommend only when useful: to address a meaningful issue, resolve ambiguity, reduce evidence-supported risk, improve fitness for purpose, or enable a decision. Each recommendation should:

- connect to a finding;
- explain the intended outcome or value;
- be specific and proportionate;
- avoid invented owners, deadlines, constraints, or certainty;
- be labeled as general guidance unless it is a source-established action.

Prioritize recommendations by impact, urgency, dependency, or sequence when that improves actionability. Do not create recommendations merely to populate a section.
